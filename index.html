Why it's different

Real wood. Simple swaps.

Solid alderwood

Each 2.75-inch disc is cut and sanded from solid alder, then scented with fragrance oil and cured before it's sealed.

Magnetic, tool-free

A steel plate set flush in the back of the disc snaps onto two magnets on the visor clip. Swapping scents takes seconds.

Sealed until you open it

Every disc is heat-sealed in its own foil pouch, so refills stay sealed until you're ready for them.

Questions

Good to know

How does it attach?

The steel clip slides onto your sun visor. Two magnets on the clip hold the disc by the steel plate set into its back.

How do I change scents?

Pull the disc off the clip and set a new one on. A Refill 3-Pack has three sealed discs, and you can mix or match scents.
