About Nº01

Designed for your escape.

Nº01 makes car fresheners from solid alderwood: a scented disc that snaps onto a magnetic visor clip. We're a student company in BYU-Idaho's Integrated Business Core, and we make every disc by hand in Rexburg, Idaho.

How we make it

Four steps, all by hand.

  1. No01

    Cut

    We rip and crosscut alder blanks, then bandsaw and sand each one into a 2.75-inch round.

  2. No02

    Set

    A steel plate is set flush into the back of every disc, so it holds to the clip's magnets.

  3. No03

    Scent

    Each disc is weighed, dosed with fragrance oil and cured in a sealed tub for 24 hours.

  4. No04

    Seal

    After a quality check, every disc is heat-sealed in its own foil pouch and logged by lot.

Packed by hand

Simple packaging, nothing extra.

Every order is packed by our team.

  • Starter Kit: one scented disc and the magnetic visor clip in a kraft box
  • Refill 3-Pack: three sealed discs; mix or match scents
  • Gift Bundle: the kit and a 3-pack together in a gift box
