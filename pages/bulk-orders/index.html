Questions

Business order FAQ

What logo file do you need?

A vector file (SVG, AI, EPS or PDF) works best. A high-resolution PNG also works. We'll show you a proof before we engrave anything.

Is there a setup fee?

No. The price per unit includes the engraving and the clip.

How do we pay?

We send an invoice. Pay 50% when you approve your proof and the rest on delivery.

Can you make more than 250?

Right now we make up to 250 per order. For more, we can split it into deliveries. Ask in your quote request.

How long does the scent last?

We're testing that in real cars now and will share the results. We won't promise a number until we've measured it.
